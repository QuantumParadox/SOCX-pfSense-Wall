# SOCX v0.5 Research Notes

This pass focused on applying pfSense operator patterns to SOCX without changing firewall policy automatically.

## Applied Patterns

- pfBlockerNG/DNSBL: review repeated domains and likely app/vendor false positives before exact FQDN allowlisting.
- Suricata/IDS: separate high-signal malware/C2/exploit alerts from routine decoder/stream noise before suppression or thresholding.
- Zeek: treat crash diagnostic folders under `/var/spool/zeek/tmp` as health evidence, not just clutter.
- pfSense PHP service status: validate package service definitions before calling service status functions, catching malformed service entries early.
